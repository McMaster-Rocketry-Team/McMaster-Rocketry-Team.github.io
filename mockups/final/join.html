How it works

Four steps.

The whole process takes about two weeks and there is no interview panel — just a conversation about what you want to work on.

  3. Have a chat

Straight answers

The things
people ask.

If your question is not here, email us. We would rather answer it than have you talk yourself out of applying.

I have never built anything. Can I still join?

Yes, and most of us started there. First-term members are paired with someone who has done the job before, and your first deliverable is deliberately small. The team exists to teach this.

I am not in engineering. Is there a place for me?

Yes. Business, Outreach and Finance are full subteams, not afterthoughts — they run sponsorship, the budget, and everything we do on campus. Commerce, communications, humanities and science students all sit on them. Payload also takes science students most years.

How much time does it actually take?

About six hours a week for most members, rising in the four weeks before a launch and dropping to almost nothing over exams. We plan around the academic term. TODO: confirm the real figure

Can I join partway through the year?

Usually yes. Email us and we will tell you honestly whether a subteam has room right now, rather than putting you on a list.

What if I want to try a different subteam later?

Normal, and encouraged. Plenty of people move once they have seen what the other subteams actually do.
