Tiers

What you get.

Three tiers, and a fourth option if you would rather give materials or machine time than money. That is often more useful to us.

In-kind support. Machine time, carbon, connectors, test equipment, PCB runs, or an afternoon of an engineer’s time are frequently worth more to us than the equivalent cash. Tell us what you have and we will tell you honestly whether we can use it.

We hire first years.

We hire people who have never touched a rocket. The only thing we ask is that you want to find out how high this one goes.

Find us at Clubsfest or Facultyfest at the start of term, or apply any time. You do not need to be an engineer: we need people on operations as much as on airframe.
