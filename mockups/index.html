Day 1 · Design exploration

Three directions.

Same content, same palette — McMaster Maroon with white, grey and black. The only variable is treatment. Each direction comps a hero, the six-rocket fleet lineup, and the seven-subsystem grid, and all six run your real logo and real Launch Canada 2025 footage. A, B and C are the three primary directions; C.1, C.2 and C.3 push C in three different ways. Open them and compare.

The Hybrid

C’s editorial voice and honest data treatment, on B’s structural discipline, with C.2’s closing panel as a standing call to action. Seven real pages, not one comp.

Includes: home, rockets index, a per-vehicle template you refill each year, subteams, members, join, and sponsors — with a real mobile menu, a tier table and contact routes throughout.

Flight Profile

Real assets are in. Logo vectorised to a 1.8 KB SVG that recolours via currentColor (white on A and C, maroon on B). Hero video cut from your Launch Canada 2025 clip — 12 s from ignition, cropped to remove the “LAUNCH CANADA 2025” watermark, silent, 2.8 MB. Fleet rockets in A and B are still SVG placeholders; C plots real measured apogees and draws the three vehicles with no verified record as dashed gaps rather than guesses. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion.

All six have been through an accessibility and mobile pass. Each now has a real mobile menu, a skip link, a visible focus ring, a pause control on the background video, a 720p cut served to small screens, and an apogee chart that reflows to horizontal bars below 700px.
